## Tokens

### Color Roles

| Role | Token | Usage | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| surface | `--color-surface` | Primary app surface. | Full-background brand wash. |
| panel | `--color-panel` | Tool panels and inspectors. | Nested decorative cards. |
| accent | `--color-accent` | Primary action and active state. | Painting every control. |
| warning | `--color-warning` | Pending, stale or degraded state. | Hiding warnings in neutral gray. |
| danger | `--color-danger` | Failure or destructive action. | Neutral warning states. |
| success | `--color-success` | Verified or complete state. | Success before evidence exists. |
