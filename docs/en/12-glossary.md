# Glossary

This glossary keeps internal terms out of the main reading path. Explain the idea first. Use the canonical name when needed.

## Product SOT

Human translation: product truth.

Defines what will be delivered, for whom, within which limits, and which proof ends the argument. Common mistake: treating Product SOT as a pretty summary instead of a scope contract.

## Full Product SOT Scope Coverage

Every important promise must be planned, blocked, out of scope, delegated to a human, or proven. Common mistake: implementing the easy slice and calling it the full product.

## Method Contract

Contract connecting route, method, gates, workers, and evidence. Common mistake: choosing a method that does not change proof.

## Worker Packet

Bounded task delivered to a worker. It should contain source, scope, out-of-scope, authority, and proof.

## Worker Result

Structured worker return. It should contain evidence, findings, blockers, and handoff.

## Gate Report

Report explaining advance, block, and unblock conditions.

## Receipt Five

Completion receipt in five questions: request, delivery, evidence, review, remaining risk.

## Human Gate

Material operator decision with readable packet. Not “approve this?”.

## Readback

Real inspection of produced artifact. The factory reads, opens, or verifies before believing.

## No-idle

Guard against silent stalling. It is not a second Hermes and does not invent authority.

## Learnback

Learning promoted with proof: test, doc, skill, worker, gate, issue, or process change.

## Product Face

Proof of visible product experience. Not decorative screenshots.

## Capability Pack

Specialized coverage for product type or surface. Common mistake: assuming capability without active pack.

## Sealed Source Envelope

Protection of original source before interpretation.

## Universal Signal Intake

Universal intake of signal/source before route selection.

## Specialist Research Decision

Specialist research decision when work requires knowledge or evidence beyond the common path.

## Product Creation Plan

Product creation plan derived from product truth.

## Product Implementation Readiness

Gate verifying whether product is ready to become safe implementation.

## Factory v1 Completion Gate

Closure gate preventing completion without reconciliation.

## Hermes Kanban

Live runtime floor: cards, states, dependencies, attachments, and transitions.

## factoryctl

Primary Factory CLI.

## route classes, method engines, operating-system areas

Public contracts classifying work, method, and operating areas.

## product_creation

Canonical route ID. Do not translate it when used as an ID.
